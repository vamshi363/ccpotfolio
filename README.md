# Creator Circle — Script Portfolio (`ccpotfolio`)

A retro typewriter-themed scriptwriting portfolio showcasing 11 high-retention long-form and viral short-form video scripts with interactive hook selectors, mood board styling, and typewriter paper strip aesthetics.

🌐 **Live Website**: [https://vamshi363.github.io/ccpotfolio/](https://vamshi363.github.io/ccpotfolio/)  
📁 **Repository**: [https://github.com/vamshi363/ccpotfolio](https://github.com/vamshi363/ccpotfolio)

---

## 🖋️ Featured Scripts

1. **The 24-Jam Experiment** — Choice overload & decision paralysis psychology.
2. **Your Phone Is Interrupting You** — The dopamine and notification loop.
3. **The Invisible Cost of Card Payments** — Pain of paying & behavioral economics.
4. **Make It Yours** — The IKEA effect & endowment bias.
5. **Stop Rereading Your Notes. Do This Instead.** — Active recall vs passive review.
6. **The Raise Trap** — Lifestyle creep and hedonic adaptation.
7. **Bedtime Procrastination** — Revenge bedtime procrastination breakdown.
8. **Flipped Interaction** — Audience engagement dynamics.
9. **Before I Stood Up** — Personal narrative & vulnerability storytelling.
10. **How It Ended** — Narrative resolution & climax engineering.
11. **Why “I’ll Start When I’m Ready” Is a Trap** — Perfectionism vs action bias.

---

## 🎨 Design & Features

- **Retro Typewriter Aesthetic**: Lined paper background pattern, Courier Prime typewriter typography, vintage stamps, and physical card textures.
- **Interactive Hook Variations**: Alternate hooks for A/B testing viewer retention and CTR.
- **Mood Board & Visual Direction**: Audio cues, pacing guidance, and B-roll notes embedded within each script.
- **Zero Dependencies**: Pure HTML5, CSS3, and modern vanilla JavaScript.
